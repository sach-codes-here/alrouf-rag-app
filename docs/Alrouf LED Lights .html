Installation 

The Alrouf LED Panel Light is a slim, high-uniformity luminaire designed for modern offices, hospitals, meeting rooms, retail spaces, and educational facilities. It delivers comfortable, low-glare illumination with balanced brightness and color consistency to support productive, pleasant interiors.

Suitable for recessed or surface mounting in modular ceilings, it offers long lifetime, high efficacy, and a clean, minimalist appearance. The panels are available in multiple IP ratings (IP20/IP44/IP65) to meet various indoor environmental requirements.

Features

  • Highly energy efficient – up to 114 lm/w
  • Rated nominal lifetime: 50,000 hours
  • Quick and easy installation
  • Protection: IP20
  • Operating temperature: -20°C ~ +50°C
  • No UV & IR; completely mercury-free
  • Warranty: 5 years
  • Certificates: CE (EMC, RoHS)
